OJIX LOGO KIT
=============

COLORS
  Deep Navy      #0F1C2E
  Signal Orange  #C9662F
  Steel Blue     #8FAAC9  (tagline on navy)
  White          #FFFFFF

TYPEFACE
  Montserrat Bold (700) — wordmark
  Montserrat Medium (500), ~0.4em tracking — tagline

/ (vector, SVG)
  ojix-primary-dark.svg .................. full lockup, navy background
  ojix-primary-light.svg ................. full lockup, white background
  ojix-primary-transparent-ondark.svg .... full lockup, no background, for dark placements
  ojix-primary-transparent-onlight.svg ... full lockup, no background, for light placements
  ojix-mono-white.svg .................... all white, transparent
  ojix-mono-black.svg .................... all black, transparent
  ojix-wordmark-*.svg .................... wordmark + rule, no tagline
  ojix-icon-navy / orange / white.svg .... square mark, 512
  ojix-appicon-navy-rounded.svg .......... rounded app icon, 512

/png (raster, 3-4x)
  full lockups, wordmarks, icons, favicon-512/192/180/64/32/16

USE
  Clear space on all sides = height of the orange rule.
  Minimum width: full lockup 180px / 45mm, wordmark 90px / 24mm, icon 16px / 6mm.
  On mid-tone or photographic backgrounds use the monochrome versions.
  Do not recolor, stretch, outline, or add effects to the wordmark.
